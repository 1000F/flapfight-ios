Files changed:

**Learnings for future iterations:**
- Phase accumulation (var phase: Double = 0.0, phase += 2π*freq/sr) is critical for smooth frequency transitions
- Without phase accumulation, frequency sweeps would have audible clicks/pops at discontinuities
- Exponential envelopes (pow(1-t, 2)) sound more natural than linear (1-t) for attack/decay
- For chords, normalize by dividing by frequency count to prevent clipping when summing waveforms
- Vibrato uses frequency modulation: modulatedFreq = baseFreq + sin(2π*vibRate*t) * vibDepth
- All synthesis methods still use pre-rendered PCM buffers (AVAudioPCMBuffer) initialized once at startup
- No audio files needed - everything is still fully procedural
- Sample rate (sr) of 44100 is standard for iOS audio
